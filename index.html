the problem

A model can look right in a notebook and fail in production. A dashboard can disagree with its source. A plan can miss a material and nobody notices until the line stops.

who is writing

I'm a Data Scientist at one of Indonesia's largest halal beauty and personal-care manufacturers. I test before I quote, so each project here has self-checks, reconciliation or tests, and says what it leaves out.

the plan

  1. Frame the decisionWhat are we maximising, and what can't we break?
  2. Build it and prove itA solver, explicit constraints, and checks that fail loudly.
  3. Ship itA dashboard, a scheduled report, a plan people can use.

Without this, stock expires and demand goes unserved. With it, a plan covers demand with fewer machines and a page matches its source.
